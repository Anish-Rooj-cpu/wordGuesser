📖 How to Play

Goal: Reveal all your team's words before the others do.

  • 2 teams: 5×5 board — 8 words per team, 8 yellow neutrals, 1 assassin. 3 teams: 6×6 — 8 per team, 10 neutrals, 2 assassins. 4 teams: 7×7 — 9 per team, 10 neutrals, 3 assassins.
  • The Spymaster sees all colours and gives ONE word (max 20 letters) plus a number (e.g. "Ocean - 2"), only when their team has no guesses left. The word cannot be a word on the board.
  • The Guessers get exactly that many guesses. Your own team's card: keep going. Another team's card or a yellow neutral: your turn ends (a neutral gives no penalty and no point).
  • Assassin: your team is eliminated; the other teams keep playing.
  • A team wins by revealing all its words (whoever revealed the last one) or by being the last team not eliminated.
  • Guessers of all teams can chat; Spymasters can only send hints.
