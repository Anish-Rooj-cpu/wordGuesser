📖 How to Play

Goal: Be the first team to guess all your team's words.

  • The Spymaster sees the true colors of all 25 cards (Red, Blue, Black, or Neutral).
  • The Guessers only see the words and must try to guess which cards belong to their team based on the Spymaster's hints.
  • The Spymaster gives a 1-word hint and a number (e.g., "Ocean - 2"). The word must relate to cards on the board, and the number tells the guessers how many cards relate to that hint.
  • Guessers can make up to N + 1 guesses (where N is the number given by the Spymaster).
  • If a guesser selects the other team's card or a neutral card, their turn ends immediately.
  • If a guesser selects the Assassin (Black card), their team instantly loses!

Spymaster Restrictions:

  • The hint must be exactly one word.
  • You cannot use any word (or part of a word) that is currently unrevealed on the board.
